    ⚖️ What is Mass?
    Learn about mass and how we measure it

    🍬 Mass = How heavy something is

    Mass tells us how much matter is in an object.
    We measure mass in grams (g) and kilograms (kg).

    🍭 · 🧁 · 🍬 · 🎂 · 🍩

    A feather is very light — it has a small mass.
    A big cake is heavy — it has a large mass.

    💡 Remember!

    g

    Grams (g) — for light objects. Example: sweets, muffins, pencils.

    kg

    Kilograms (kg) — for heavier objects. Example: bags, people, watermelons.

    !

    1 kg = 1 000 g — one kilogram equals one thousand grams!

    🍑 Fruity Puzzle
    Exercise 13.1 · Question 1

    🛒 Read the problem

    📖

    Suzie buys two peaches and four pears.

    One peach weighs 80 grams.

    Four pears weigh the same as two peaches.

    How much does one pear weigh?

    🍑 🍑   🍐 🍐 🍐 🍐
    🤔
    grams

    🧠 Work it out — step by step

    1

    Two peaches weigh:

    2 × 80 g = ?
    g
    2

    Four pears = two peaches, so four pears weigh:

    4 pears = ? g

    (This equals the same as two peaches!)

    3

    So one pear weighs:

    ? ÷ 4 = ?
    g

    ✅ Great work! Here is the full solution:

    🍑 One peach = 80 g
    🍑🍑 Two peaches = 2 × 80 = 160 g
    🍐🍐🍐🍐 Four pears = 160 g (same as two peaches)
    🍐 One pear = 160 ÷ 4 = 40 g

    How close was your estimate?

    🧁 Muffin Scales
    Exercise 13.1 · Question 2

    🍫 Read the problem

    The chocolate bar has a mass of 100 g.

    The scales are balanced — both sides weigh the same!

    Left side: 4 muffins  |  Right side: 2 muffins + chocolate bar

    a) What is the mass of one muffin? 🧁

    Because the scales are balanced:

    4 muffins = 2 muffins + 100 g
    (subtract 2 muffins from both sides)
    2 muffins = ?
    1

    If 2 muffins = 100 g, then 1 muffin = ?

    g

    b) What is the mass of each side? ⚖️

    We know 1 muffin = 50 g.

    The left side has 4 muffins. What is the total mass of the left side?

    g

    ✅ Excellent! Full solution:

    ⚖️ Both sides balanced: 4 muffins = 2 muffins + 100 g
    → 4 − 2 = 2 muffins = 100 g
    🧁 1 muffin = 100 ÷ 2 = 50 g

    Left side: 4 × 50 g = 200 g
    Right side: 2 × 50 g + 100 g = 100 + 100 = 200 g ✓
    🧁🧁 🧁🧁 = 200 g
